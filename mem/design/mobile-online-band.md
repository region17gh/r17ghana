name: Compact online band on phones
description: The Attend online band must stay a two-line bar on phones; the serif title is desktop-only
type: design
---

On phones the "Attend online" band stays a compact two-line bar: the gold
eyebrow, the one-line timing note, and the gold button. The long serif title
("Watch the Ghana-Chicago Business Roundtable online") shows only where there
is room for it, and is hidden on narrow screens, where the band keeps its
accessible name through an aria-label on the section.

The user asked for this on 2 Nov 2026 after measuring the band at 239px, a
third of a 402x725 phone screen. It is now 80px (11%) on that screen, 99px at
360px wide, 118px at 320px wide.

Any future sticky band on a mobile page follows the same rule: it must not
take more than roughly a tenth of the screen, because the primary audience is
on metered mobile connections.
